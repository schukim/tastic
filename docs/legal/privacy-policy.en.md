# Tastic Privacy Policy

**Effective date: June 20, 2026** *(update at launch)*

Tastic ("the Service") respects your privacy and complies with applicable data protection laws. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

---

## 1. Personal Data We Collect

**(1) Sign-up & authentication**
- Email address
- For social login (Google, and Apple later): social account identifier, email, profile information

**(2) Profile setup**
- Nickname, profile image (optional), interest categories, language preference

**(3) Data created while using the Service**
- Review text and interview answers you write
- Information about content you reviewed (title, creator, etc.)
- Taste analysis results, recommendation history, service usage logs

**(4) Paid membership purchases (planned)**
- Subscription and payment status. Payment instrument details (e.g., card numbers) are handled by Google Play / App Store and the payment processor; the Service does not store them.

**(5) Automatically collected data**
- Error logs and device/app environment information, for stability and diagnostics.

---

## 2. Purposes of Use

- User identification, authentication, and service delivery
- Generating personal reviews via interviews, taste analysis, and content recommendations
- Providing membership-tier features and managing usage
- Service improvement, error diagnosis, and customer support
- Compliance with legal obligations

---

## 3. Third-Party Sharing & Sub-processors

To provide our features we entrust data processing to the parties below. **Notably, the review and interview text you write is sent to the AI providers below for AI analysis and generation.**

| Sub-processor | Purpose | Data transferred |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase, Inc. | Authentication, database, server infrastructure | Account and service usage data |
| DeepSeek (Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Co., Ltd.) | AI question / review / taste-analysis generation | Review & interview text, reviewed content info |
| OpenAI, L.L.C. | Content identification & recommendation (incl. web search) | Content titles/metadata, recommendation context |
| Google LLC | Social login (OAuth) | Auth identifier, email, profile |
| Sentry, Inc. | Crash & error diagnostics | Error/crash logs, device & OS info, user identifier |
| RevenueCat, Inc. / Google LLC / Apple Inc. (later) | Payment & subscription processing | Subscription identifier, payment status |

We impose data-protection obligations on sub-processors as required by law.

## 4. International Data Transfers

We transfer personal data internationally as described below. By signing up and using the Service, you are deemed to consent to these transfers.

| Recipient | Country | Data | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supabase, Inc. | Australia (Sydney) | Account and service data | Infrastructure & storage |
| DeepSeek | China | Review & interview text, content info | AI generation & analysis |
| OpenAI, L.L.C. | United States | Content titles & context | Content identification & recommendation |
| Sentry, Inc. | United States | Error/crash logs, device & OS info | Crash & error diagnostics |

> ⚠️ Please note that the impressions and reviews you enter for AI generation are transmitted to the providers above (including those located in China and the United States). We recommend not entering sensitive personal information.

---

## 5. Data Retention

- Upon account deletion, we delete your personal data without undue delay, except where retention is required by law.
- You can request account deletion and data removal at any time via the in-app "Delete Account" feature.
- Deletion method: electronic files are permanently erased in an unrecoverable manner.

---

## 6. Your Rights

You may at any time:
- Request access to or correction of your personal data
- Request deletion of, or restriction of processing of, your data
- Delete your account (in-app: My Page → Delete Account)

You can exercise these rights through in-app features or the contact below.

---

## 7. Security Measures

- Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Row-Level Security (RLS) access control — users can access only their own data
- Token-based access management and least-privilege access

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

The Service does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 14. If we learn that such data has been collected, we delete it without delay.
