앱: Tastic · 개발자/운영자: 정태우 · 최종 갱신: 2026년 7월 8일

이 페이지는 Tastic 이용자가 계정과 관련 데이터의 삭제를 요청하는 방법을 안내합니다. 아래 두 가지 방법 중 편한 방법으로 요청하실 수 있으며, 두 방법 모두 결과는 동일하게 계정과 데이터가 영구 삭제됩니다.

방법 1. 앱 내에서 직접 삭제 (권장)

  1. Tastic 앱을 실행하고 로그인합니다.
  2. 하단 탭에서 마이(👤) 화면으로 이동합니다.
  3. 계정 삭제를 선택합니다.
  4. 안내에 따라 삭제를 확인하면 계정과 데이터가 즉시 영구 삭제됩니다.

⚠️ 계정 삭제는 되돌릴 수 없습니다. 작성하신 평론·인터뷰 기록, 취향 분석 결과가 모두 함께 삭제됩니다.

방법 2. 이메일로 삭제 요청

앱에 접근할 수 없거나 직접 요청을 원하시면 아래 이메일로 요청해 주세요. 본인 확인을 위해 가입에 사용한 이메일 주소로 보내주시기 바랍니다.

Tastic Account & Data Deletion

App: Tastic · Developer/Operator: Taewoo Cheong · Last updated: July 8, 2026

This page explains how Tastic users can request deletion of their account and associated data. You can use either of the two methods below; both permanently delete your account and data.

Method 1. Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open the Tastic app and sign in.
  2. Go to the My (👤) tab in the bottom navigation.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion. Your account and data are permanently deleted immediately.

⚠️ Account deletion is irreversible. All of your reviews, interview records, and taste analysis results are deleted together.

Method 2. Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app or prefer to request manually, email us below. Please send from the email address used to sign up so we can verify you.

Request deletion by email

To: tastic2026@gmail.com · Please put “Account Deletion Request” in the subject. We process requests within 7 business days and reply when done.

Data that is deleted

Once your request is processed, the following data is permanently erased in an unrecoverable manner.

CategoryDetails
AccountEmail, social login identifier, authentication account
ProfileNickname, profile image, interest categories, language preference
Reviews & interviewsReview text you wrote, interview answers, reviewed-content records
Taste & recommendationsTaste analysis results, recommendation history

Data that is retained & retention period

As a rule, we delete all personal data without undue delay upon request. However, the following may be retained on a limited basis where required by law or for service operation.

CategoryReasonRetention period
Payment/subscription records (if any)Retention required by applicable e-commerce/consumer-protection lawPeriod set by law (e.g., up to 5 years for payment records)
Error/crash logsService stability diagnostics (used statistically after removing personal identifiers)Auto-deleted after up to 90 days

Aside from the above, your in-service personal data — account, reviews, profile, taste, and usage records — is fully removed immediately upon deletion and not retained thereafter. Logs kept by payment processors (e.g., Google Play) and AI/infrastructure sub-processors follow each provider's own retention policy.
